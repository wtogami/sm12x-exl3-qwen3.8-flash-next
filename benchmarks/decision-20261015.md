## Findings
1. **Behavior is saturated from K4 upward.** PQA spread 0.6pt, IFEval 1.6pt with no bit-monotonicity, BCB rises 3->5 then saturates (K5==K6==31.1), seven noise-dominated, core gates saturated. SQNR climbs 2.8dB/bit buys NO measurable behavior above ~4 bits.
2. **Calibration axis (K6V vs K6, one-variable): wash on every published gate.** Only behavioral difference attributable to corpus: panel calibration's longer chains-of-thought cause A3 truncation at max_tokens. => corpus pick: **vanilla** (biomed-clean, gate-equal).
3. **Profile axis (4.25bpw mixed vs K4/K5 uniform): mixed-profile at 4.25 matches K6 on every gate** (BCB 29.7, IFE 84.5, biomed miss is C1, not truncation) — independent corroboration that allocation tuning, not raw bits, drives quality.
4. K6V remains the serving incumbent; K6C's 12/13 (x4 deterministic) confirmed on both panel-calibrated 6-bit-class cards.

## 2026-10-17 (evening) — QUALITY PROGRAM CLOSED: vendor 4.25bpw is the terminal card (owner decision)
Decision: no further effort toward better-or-cheaper quant cards; vendor
Qwen3.8-Flash-Next EXL3 K4.25-v1 remains the serving default at TP1 and TP2.

Evidence base (all true-KLD = lm_head KL vs BF16, panel corpus, identical protocol):
- Quality band is FLAT above ~4.25 bits: uniform K5 @ 5.0 bits = 0.01412 ≈
  vendor @ 4.25 bits = 0.01433; gates/probes (PQA/IFEval/BCB + x20) put even
  K6V @ 6 bits in the same behavioral wash. Marginal bits buy zero observable
  quality.
- Reproduction FAILED: our measured-allocation programs (l2/l3 knapsack on
  panel measurements) produced 0.0215 @ 4.2465 and 0.0198 @ 4.5868 - both WORSE
  than plain uniform K4 (0.0209 @ 4.0) and 33-50% worse than vendor at similar
  bits. Additive knapsack model is 1.4-1.5x optimistic and its selections
  actively harmful. Vendor's per-expert allocation could not be reproduced
  without ~+0.75 bits (uniform K5).
- Vendor 4.25 is a geometric sweet spot: the only quality point that fits
  TP1 (73.8 GiB GPU-side, 512K-confirmed via CAPACITY-TP1.md). Uniform K5
  matches quality but needs ~80 GiB weights -> kills single-card 512K; K6V
  needs ~93.8 GiB -> no TP1 at all.
- Servicing measured-style allocations even when desired is blocked by b12x
  policy (consecutive (3,4,5)-family tiers on the fused path; docs/engine-tier-
  capability.md); the mapped 1.5-day EP-elimination route (rank-sliced format
  path) unlocks only our losing allocations - parked indefinitely.

Closed lanes: measured-card builds (measured425/459 kept as offline artifacts,
MEASURED-*-KEPT.md), multitier adapter patch (T3-T5 cancelled), tuned/heuristic
lane (closed earlier), measuredl2 recipe (unbuilt), per-expert-granularity
re-measurement (would only be worth restarting for a SUB-4.25-bit cost play,
validated against vendor's 0.01433 ground truth - not scheduled).
Residual risk accepted: vendor allocation is a published black box; fallback
if ever unavailable is uniform K5 TP2 (quality-equivalent, +18% expert VRAM).
