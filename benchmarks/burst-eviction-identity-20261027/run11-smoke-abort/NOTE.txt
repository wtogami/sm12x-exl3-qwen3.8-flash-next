Run 11 -- aborted at its own smoke gate, and that is the whole record.

driver.log holds the gate output; smoke-plain.out and smoke-question.out are the raw responses
from the two prompt modes at 8,192 tokens; ops.log is boot and teardown; verdict.tsv is a header
with no rows, because no leg ran. Nothing was hidden: the campaign ended here by design.

What the gate caught: the plain-completion mode returned no token stream. The served model is
chat-tuned, so a bare text-completion request is answered with a reasoning block or with
immediate end-of-turn, neither of which yields a comparable continuation. The driver refuses to
score an absent measurement as agreement -- the rule run 4 taught it -- so it stopped before
booting three engines.

That is the fourth distinct axis on which this test invalidated itself (batch-shape variance,
prefix caching, empty comparisons, repetition cycles, and now endpoint/chat format), and the
reason the ledger recommends stopping rather than a seventh campaign: fixing this one means
driving the chat endpoint with a single system turn and no history, and the two prior objections
still stand. Given measured cross-run variance, INCONCLUSIVE is the likely result; and since
raising utilization only reduces eviction frequency, no outcome here can argue against the 0.98
default. A real mismatch would be a defect in the preempt-and-restore path at every utilization,
reportable upstream, remedied by admission control.

Note also what these receipts do show, at no cost: the 0.96 subject legs took 637-668 s for the
workload the 0.98 legs finished in 255-258 s.

Gate transcript, verbatim from driver.log (the .log itself stays out of git by repo convention;
this is the 384-byte thing that decided the campaign):

    === smoke: both prompt modes at small depth on card 2 ===
      card 2 clear (18 MiB used)
    SMOKE-FAILED(plain): phase exited nonzero
    smoke[question]: kind=token_ids lens=[1, 64] distinct=57 evictions=0.0 tok/s=20640 prefix_hit=0.0
    smoke[question]: sequences [0] have no token stream -- refusing to score an absent measurement as agreement
    SMOKE-FAILED: aborting before any real leg boots

Read it carefully, because the two modes fail differently and only one of them is obvious. The
plain mode exited nonzero and said so. The question mode looked ALIVE -- 57 distinct tokens, a
token_ids stream, 20,640 tok/s -- and would have been waved through by any gate that checks
entropy alone. What killed it is that one of its two sequences carried no stream at all: lens
[1, 64] means one sequence produced a single token and the other 64, and the driver refuses to
call an absent measurement agreement. A cheap warm engine at 20,640 tok/s also means nothing was
ever evicted, so even a well-formed answer here would have scored nothing.
