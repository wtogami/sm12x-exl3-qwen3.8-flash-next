Serialized deep prefill on this engine runs at ~5-6k tok/s, so a 980,992-token burst
cannot finish in 4.4s. The solo phase of each leg had already prefilled the SAME prompts,
so the 'concurrent burst' was a prefix-cache hit on all four sequences: no prefill work,
no pool pressure, no eviction possible. The test's own zero-preemption result was the
artifact, not the finding -- and it would have been reported as 'eviction is safe'.

Fixes: separate reference and burst prompt sets (identical token counts, different
nonces), per-request timing and effective tok/s recorded, prefix-cache hit ratio sampled
across the burst window, and compare now returns INCONCLUSIVE (2) when a leg is
cache-defeated rather than letting it count as evidence.
