C1 A/B at 0.94 vs 0.98 (burst, exl3, v0.32, TP1, card 0) -- the receipts behind criterion (b)

Committed 2026-10-27 during cleanup, three days after the run, because the qualification ledger has
been quoting these numbers since commit ff64cf2 and nothing here was in git. The raw directory was
about to be deleted with the rest of the campaign scratch; it is preserved instead.

  util  legs  decode C1   128K prefill      261,632 prefill   seven blend  orchid   boot free
  0.94  ok    120.7      17.80 s / 7192     37.63 s / 6953    148.6        239.2    94.51/95.05 GiB
  0.98  ok    118.4      18.03 s / 7101     37.59 s / 6960    148.1        238.6    94.51/95.05 GiB

Criterion (b) -- "not measurably worse at C1" -- rests on this table: decode is 1.9% FASTER at 0.98,
128K prefill TTFT is 1.3% slower, the 261K prefill, seven-fact blend and orchid suite are within
0.3%. Two cells per row, no repeats: the spread that matters here was later shown to be batch-shape
variance, not utilization (see docs/qualification.md, "greedy decoding on this stack is not
batch-invariant"), so a difference under about 2% on a single run is not resolvable and is not
claimed as one.
